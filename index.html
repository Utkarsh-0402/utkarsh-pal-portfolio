02How it works · Process

Five steps. Every one has proof.

Tap a step to see how I approach it — and the work where I actually did it.

Start with the market and the user — not the solution.

Before proposing anything, I map who is hurting, how big the space is, and who is already playing in it.

Where I did this
Voice AI Market Research ↗ Rapido / Uber Bike Safety ↗ Accredian · Learning Product Research ↗

Turn a vague pain into sharp sub-problems.

I split a big problem into pieces a team can act on, and anchor each one to a real persona.

Where I did this
Instagram SafeConnect · 2 personas ↗ LinkedIn AI Discovery · problem framing ↗

Decide what not to build.

Good prioritisation is mostly saying no. I use simple frameworks to make the trade-offs visible to everyone.

Where I did this
LinkedIn AI Discovery · MoSCoW ↗ Maximoney · Teardown & recommendations ↗

Agree how we'll know it worked — before we build.

Every idea gets a success metric up front, so the team is judged on outcomes, not output.

Where I did this
LinkedIn AI Discovery · success metrics ↗ Instagram SafeConnect · KPIs ↗ Josh Talks · AI quality evaluation ↗

Sequence it into something a team can ship.

I turn the plan into phases and loops, so value lands early and learning shapes the next step.

Where I did this
Instagram SafeConnect · phased roadmap ↗ NPrep · 3-day engagement loop ↗
